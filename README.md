# Standart Petroleum LLP website starter

This is a responsive multi-page static website starter with a deep-green and light-green visual theme and subtle CSS animations for Standart Petroleum Limited Liability Partnership.

## Visual design
- Deep green, light green and white brand palette inspired by the provided company document.
- Subtle entrance animations, hover lift on cards, animated brand mark and decorative hero rings.
- Respects reduced-motion preferences.

## Pages
- Home
- About Us
- Our Products
- Our Services
- Why Choose Us
- FAQ
- Contact Us (buyer enquiry form)
- Privacy Policy template
- Terms of Use template

## Preview locally
Open `index.html` in a browser. For best results, use a simple local server.

## Before publishing
1. Confirm the exact registered company name and legal entity type. The name supplied for this project is used as provided.
2. Add the official logo, phone/WhatsApp, business email, office address and social links.
3. Verify and approve all statements about the refinery mandate, authorization, products and supply relationships.
4. Replace the placeholder `sales@yourdomain.com` in `assets/site.js` with the business email.
5. Connect the enquiry form to a secure form service/backend if you want enquiries submitted without requiring the visitor's email app.
6. Review the privacy policy and terms with a qualified professional.
7. Test the website on mobile and desktop before launch.

## Notes
- The design uses remote Unsplash image URLs, so an internet connection is needed to load those photographs.
- This is a static front-end starter. It does not include a database, admin dashboard, buyer login or document storage.
- Product categories are illustrative and should be edited to reflect the company's actual scope.
